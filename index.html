Why Racing X-Ray

Finding value in racing takes time and form study, every day. Racing X-Ray does the work for you, so you can keep betting your way, with value on your side.

What members get

  • Selections by email and Telegram, in good time before racing, the night before if the early prices are right, otherwise on the day, each with a minimum price and a stake in points. Only on days something meets the criteria. No filler bets.
  • A short write-up with every pick, giving the real reasoning behind it.
  • A recommended bank size and staking plan.
  • Every method tested on years of race data before it's used.
  • About ten minutes a day to read your selections and place your bets.

Is this for you?

Probably yes, if you…

  • want to bet on value and think long term
  • are happy to follow a staking plan and bet to points
  • would rather have fewer, better bets
  • want to see every result, losers included

Probably not, if you…

  • want a bet in every race, every day
  • are after a big-priced long shot to change your week
  • can't sit through a losing run, because every method has them
  • plan to pick and choose which selections to follow

Who's behind it

I'm Jeff. I started betting for a few extra quid on the side, then found a real passion for horse racing, with the betting an extra interest. Over time, I found that picking the horse I fancied off the racecard sometimes worked, but more often didn't, and I lost some money.

So I learned from it: how I bet, how much, and on what. I became far more selective, then turned to the numbers, treating racing as a science. That research, with discipline and careful analysis, has been the bedrock of my betting for 25 years.

Racing X-Ray is the service I wish I'd had: selections on value, a proper staking plan, and every result on show.
